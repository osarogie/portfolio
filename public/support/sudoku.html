Simple Sudoku Support

Help for a calmer puzzle session.

This page covers the basics of playing Simple Sudoku, explains how the controls work, and answers the most common support questions from players.

How to Play Controls FAQ Contact

How to Play

  • Fill each row with the numbers 1 through 9, without repeating any number.
  • Fill each column the same way, with no duplicates.
  • Each 3 by 3 box must also contain the numbers 1 through 9 exactly once.
  • Starting numbers are clues and cannot be changed.

Controls

  • Select a cell, then choose a number to place it.
  • Use Clear Cell to remove a value from an editable square.
  • Use New Puzzle whenever you want to start a fresh game.
  • Your completed games appear in the history area on the main screen.
Why can’t I edit a cell?

Some cells are fixed clues that come with the puzzle. Those squares are locked and cannot be changed.

Why is the keypad disabled?

The keypad only becomes active after you select an editable cell. Tap or click a blank or previously entered square first.

What counts as a mistake?

A mistake is recorded whenever a number is placed in a cell and it does not match the puzzle’s correct solution for that position.

Will my completed games be saved?

Yes. Finished puzzles are stored in the app and shown in the completed games section on the main screen.

Need more help?

If your issue is not covered here, contact the developer using the support method listed on the app store page for Simple Sudoku.

Tip: when reporting a problem, include your device type, OS version, and a short description of what happened.
